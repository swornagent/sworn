The old architecture does not:

- consume W1's one immutable `internal/baton.Snapshot`;
- use W2's common role-neutral `baton.driver/v1` contract;
- execute Planner, Implementer design, distinct Captain, resumed
  Implementer, fresh work Verifier, engine Merge, fresh assembly Verifier and
  exact integration;

- use serialized `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`;
- expose transaction-bound immutable journal snapshots, replay-stable typed
  receipts, event offsets, normalized usage, multi-run discovery or a bounded
  post-durability observer;
- keep Baton as the sole lifecycle authority; or
- prove effect-edge crash recovery, conflicting replay, uncertain-effect
  reconciliation and clean read-only verification through `test/e2e`.

The archive's mutable delivery phases, controller authority and
`runs.state_json` would recreate the second lifecycle/oracle that RC3
explicitly removes. Its events also cannot become an OTLP delivery queue.
